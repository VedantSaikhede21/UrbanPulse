Civic complaints vanish
into black holes.

Every day, municipal corporations receive thousands of reports on potholes, water leaks, garbage, and streetlight failures — routed manually, prioritized by whoever shouts loudest, and resolved without accountability.

FOR CITIZENS

No channel.
No status. No trust.

Phone calls, WhatsApp, in-person visits — reports enter a void with no acknowledgement or ETA.

FOR OFFICERS

Manual triage.
No priority.

Every complaint arrives with equal urgency. Hours lost on triage that should be spent on repairs.

FOR ADMINS

Zero city-wide
visibility.

No health score, no trending issues, no data-driven resource allocation. Only headlines.

EXISTING SYSTEMS

Glorified
CRUD forms.

FixMyStreet-style apps are complaint boxes — no AI, no routing, no analytics, no accountability.
