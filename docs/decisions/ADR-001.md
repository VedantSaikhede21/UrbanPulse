# ADR-001: Landing Hero Redesign

> Date: 2026-07-22
> Status: Accepted

## Context

The Landing Hero is the first thing every visitor sees. The current hero communicates the product's category but doesn't create an emotional response or differentiate UrbanPulse from generic AI dashboards. Our North Star requires a visitor to understand UrbanPulse in under 10 seconds — the hero is the primary vehicle for that.

Research of 7 best-in-class hero sections (Apple, Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Framer, Alche Studio, Raycast) revealed three patterns that convert: product-as-hero, metric-driven authority, and identity targeting.

## Decision

Redesign the hero around three principles:

1. **Identity-driven headline** — "For cities that build for tomorrow" instead of "AI-Powered Civic Infrastructure"
2. **Credibility metric in the hero** — Stripe-style hard number that proves trust before the visitor reads a word
3. **Product fragment** — A taste of real agent activity visible in the background, not as a separate section, to prove the product exists

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Why Rejected |
|-------------|-------------|
| Full-screen cinematic video | Slow load, reduced-motion complexity, expensive to produce. Wrong for government-tech tone. |
| Interactive map in hero | Complex to build, performance risk, distracts from message. Defer to later sprint. |
| Minimal hero with logo + tagline | Insufficient differentiation. Doesn't answer "why should I care?" |
| Keep current hero, only polish copy | Underestimates the gap. The visual needs to match the copy quality. |

## Revision 1 (Hero v2)

**Date**: 2026-07-22
**Reason**: Creative Director review scored v1 ~7.5/10 — direction approved, execution not distinctive enough.

### What Changed

1. **Visual centerpiece** is now a live pipeline visualization showing a complaint's journey from submission to resolution through 9 agent stages. This replaces the abstract background + hidden agent cards.
2. **Headline** reframed around the citizen's journey: "Built for cities that never lose a complaint" (instead of identity-driven).
3. **Credibility metric removed** — placeholder trust metrics are worse than no metrics.
4. **Secondary CTA**: "Watch Live Demo" linking to the pipeline section below.
5. **The complaint is the protagonist** — AI agents are supporting cast. The pipeline tells a complete story (upload → verify → route → resolve → notify) in under 15 seconds.

### Why

Deep research across 10 world-class hero sections (Stripe, Linear, Apple, Vercel, Framer, Alche, Arc Browser, Raycast, Pitch, Notion) revealed a universal pattern: **product-as-hero**. Every premium site shows the real interface doing real work. v1 described the product; v2 shows it.

## Revision 2 (Hero v3)

**Date**: 2026-07-22
**Reason**: Creative Director review scored v2 ~7.0/10 — good direction but needed stronger emotional arc and more authentic citizen context.

### What Changed

1. **Emotional arc added** — Waiting phase (gray, pulsing dots) → UrbanPulse activation (bright green checkmark, self-drawing) → Resolution (before/after photos, "Resolved" badge).
2. **Authentic citizen context** — "Ward 12, Navi Mumbai", "Sector 17, NMMC", "Roads Dept" in evidence. Date shows today's date dynamically.
3. **Subtitle finalized** — "You reported it. Watch it get resolved." (scored 28/30 on clarity, emotion, memorability, differentiation, citizen-first, 5-second comprehension).
4. **Waiting phase compressed** — 2 stages (Submitted + Waiting...) instead of 3, giving more visual weight to the pipeline.
5. **Transition strengthened** — State change: card border shifts to lime, "Connected" badge appears, checkmark draws itself.
6. **Before/After evidence** — Inline SVG illustrations replace placeholder gradients. Shows repair verification.
7. **Reduced motion support** — `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses animation to final resolved state.

### Why

The waiting → resolution arc creates a genuine emotional contrast. Users feel the relief of transparency. The citizen-first language ("You reported it") cues the interactive timeline below.

## Revision 3 (Hero v4) - FROZEN

**Date**: 2026-07-22
**Status**: FROZEN

### Final Changes

1. **Badge text contrast fixed** — Changed from `text-text-tertiary` to `text-foreground` for 4.5:1+ contrast ratio.
2. **Live badge semantics** — Shows "X complaints tracked today" or "Pilot demo · Navi Mumbai" with no error state exposed.
3. **Mobile header flex** — Changed to `flex-col sm:flex-row` for better mobile wrapping.
4. **Props simplified** — Removed `uhsError`, `uhsScore`, `ticketError` props; badge shows static "Pilot demo" text.
