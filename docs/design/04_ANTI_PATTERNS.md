# Anti-Patterns

Knowing what not to build is as important as knowing what to build.

---

## Layout

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ Huge walls of text | Citizens won't read them |
| ❌ 15 cards in a grid | Choice paralysis, no hierarchy |
| ❌ Centered everything | Lacks structure, looks amateur |
| ❌ Huge empty spaces | Feels unfinished, not premium |
| ❌ Hidden navigation | Users can't find what they need |
| ❌ Tiny click targets | Fails accessibility, frustrates users |
| ❌ Duplicated CTAs | Confuses the user's next step |
| ❌ Cards with cards with cards | Infinite nesting, no information architecture |

---

## Visual

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ Random gradients | Looks like a template, no purpose |
| ❌ Floating particles everywhere | Distracting, no meaning |
| ❌ More than 3 accent colors | Visual chaos, no brand identity |
| ❌ Inconsistent border-radius | Feels unpolished, sloppy |
| ❌ Inconsistent shadows | No coherent depth system |
| ❌ Inconsistent spacing | No rhythm, hard to scan |
| ❌ Generic dashboard look | No personality, forgettable |

---

## Animation & Motion

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ Scrolling just for scrolling | Motion sickness, no purpose |
| ❌ Meaningless animations | Distracting, increases cognitive load |
| ❌ Loading screens everywhere | Feels slow, breaks flow |
| ❌ Floating particles everywhere | Tired trend, no connection to content |

---

## Content & Copy

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ AI buzzwords | Undermines trust, feels gimmicky |
| ❌ "Revolutionary" / "Cutting-edge" | Everyone says this, means nothing |
| ❌ Tech jargon for citizens | They don't care about your stack |
| ❌ Feature lists without benefits | "What" without "why it matters" |

## Interaction

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ Hover-only interactions | Broken on mobile |
| ❌ Auto-playing media | Users hate this universally |
| ❌ Scroll-jacking | Breaks user control |
| ❌ Over-engineered micro-interactions | Delays the real action |

---

## Architecture

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| ❌ "Vibe coded" layouts | No structure, impossible to maintain |
| ❌ Every page is a different layout | No consistency, users get lost |
| ❌ Too many font sizes | No typography scale |
| ❌ Too many component types | No design system |
