# Product Vision

## One-line
UrbanPulse AI transforms civic issue reporting from an opaque government portal into a transparent, observable AI-powered experience.

## How We Communicate This
Every page should make the visitor feel like they're watching UrbanPulse solve a real civic problem in front of them — not scrolling a website.

## Design Principles
1. **Observable AI** — Never hide what the system is doing. Show agents thinking, processing, deciding.
2. **Cinematic scroll** — Scrolling should feel like a product demo, not a carousel.
3. **Trust through transparency** — Government tech needs trust indicators everywhere.
4. **Premium but approachable** — Linear-level polish with civic warmth.
5. **Mobile-first storytelling** — The pipeline, dashboards, and maps must work on a phone.
