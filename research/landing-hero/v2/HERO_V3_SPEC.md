# Hero v3 Spec

Based on Creative Director feedback: the hero must communicate three layers simultaneously, not just one complaint.

---

## The Three Layers

### Layer 1 — Category (2 seconds)
> What is UrbanPulse?

The headline must establish the product category immediately — before the complaint journey, before the AI proof.

Headline options being considered:
- "From complaint to resolution. Every step visible."
- "The civic operations platform."
- "Your city's infrastructure, visible in real time."

### Layer 2 — Outcome (5 seconds)
> Why does it matter?

One specific complaint journey. The protagonist. A pothole at 5th Main Road, Ward 12, tracked through every stage: reported → AI analyzed → officer assigned → repaired → AI verified → citizen notified. Total time: 2h 33m.

This is the visual centerpiece. It demonstrates transparency, speed, and accountability.

### Layer 3 — Proof (10 seconds)
> How does it work?

The AI agents are visible but support the story rather than becoming it. Key stages only: AI analyzing (Vision), Geo routing, AI verification. No jargon. The agents are the engine, not the headline.

---

## Motion Design

- **Play once**: The complaint journey animates from submission to resolution on load. Not a loop.
- **Stop when resolved**: The animation settles on the resolved state.
- **Replay on hover**: Hovering the timeline replays the animation. Intentional, not distracting.
- **One accent glow**: Only the active stage/ticket carries the brand lime glow. Everything else stays calm.

---

## Visual Language

- **No decorative glows** in the background. Use light/shadow only to guide attention.
- **Restrained typography**: Two type personalities max. Serif for headline emphasis, sans for everything else.
- **A single moving element** (the complaint/ticket) carries the accent color.

---

## Key Differentiator

The hero must express why UrbanPulse is unique:

> Cities finally know where every complaint is, who owns it, and what happened to it — in real time.

Not "we have 9 AI agents." Not "we fix potholes." The operating system for civic infrastructure.

---

## 5-Second Test

After 5 seconds, a visitor should be able to answer:
1. What is UrbanPulse? (civic operations platform)
2. Why should I care? (complaints no longer disappear)
3. Why is it different? (total transparency, AI-powered, real time)
4. Can I trust it? (looks like a real product, not a demo)
